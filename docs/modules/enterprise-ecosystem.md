# Module spec: Enterprise scale and ecosystem (Phase 15)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | In progress: 15.1–15.4 done |
| **Plan** | `docs/PHASE_PLAN.md`, Phase 15 |

Phase 15 brings larger customers, partners and an ecosystem. Most of it is software built and tested here. Some parts are not code and stay with the business: the SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certification, partner certification, the first enterprise tenant, and store listings of native apps. The sprints below also say where an outside service needs credentials that do not exist in development; the integration is then built against a local sandbox adapter, the same way the GST network was in Phase 3.

## Sprint plan

| Sprint | Scope |

| 15.4 | **White-label and partners**: branding (logo, colours, product name), custom domain with DNS verification, email and SMS sender identities, partners (resellers / implementers), partner-managed tenants, commissions on subscription revenue, partner portal |
| 15.5 | **Marketplace and automation**: connector framework and app marketplace (install, configure, run, logs) with connectors for Tally, Shopify, marketplace orders, IndiaMART leads, Shiprocket, bank host-to-host and payment gateways (sandbox adapters where credentials are needed), a typed public SDK, and low-code workflow automation (trigger → conditions → actions) |

## 15.1 Enterprise finance (done)

Migration 0065 adds `intercompany_links`, `intercompany_transactions`, `consolidation_groups`, `treasury_instruments` and `allocation_rules` (schema `enterprise.ts`). The chart template gains system accounts that existing companies receive on first use: inter-company receivables (5440) and payables (3370), inter-company income (6550) and charges (9596), term loans (2110), interest accrued on borrowings (3380), interest on term loans (8530), fixed deposits (5520), interest accrued on deposits (5530), margin money (5540) and investments in subsidiaries (4300). Code: `backend/src/modules/enterprise/` (`finance-rules.ts` is pure and unit tested). Permissions: `finance.group.manage`, `finance.group.read`, `finance.treasury.manage`, `finance.treasury.read`. Pages: `/accounting/intercompany`, `/accounting/consolidation`, `/accounting/treasury`, `/accounting/allocations`.

**Inter-company.** Each company of the group is linked to the party that stands for it in the other companies' books. A transaction (charge, sale, loan, repayment, other) is posted at once in both: the sender is owed by the receiver (inter-company receivable against the receiver's party, with inter-company income or the chosen account), and the receiver owes the sender (inter-company payable, with inter-company charges or the chosen account). Loans and repayments move money, so a bank account is required in each company. The reconciliation shows, for each pair, what each company holds against the other on a date; the two should cancel out.

**Consolidation** (Ind AS 110, full consolidation; Schedule III Division II layout). A group has a parent and subsidiaries, each with the share held, the cost of the investment and the acquisition date. The statements add the members' balances line by line after removing every ledger line against a group company's party and the other side of inter-company journals. The parent's investment is replaced by goodwill (cost less the parent's share of the subsidiary's net assets on acquisition; a negative figure is a capital reserve). The non-controlling interest is its share of each subsidiary's net assets in equity, and its share of the subsidiary's profit below the profit line. Owners' equity is share capital of the parent and other equity. Year-end closing journals are left out of the profit and loss. If inter-company balances do not match, the difference left after elimination is shown. Not covered yet: unrealised profit on inter-company stock, other currencies, associates and joint ventures (equity method).

**Treasury.** Term loans (EMI or bullet) with their repayment schedule, outstanding principal and next instalment; fixed deposits compounded quarterly or monthly, or at simple interest, with today's value and maturity value; non-fund facilities with the LCs and BGs drawn on them (an LC or BG is refused if the facility does not have room), margin money and expiry within 30 days. Posting the month's interest makes one journal per instrument (loans: interest on term loans against interest accrued; deposits: interest accrued against interest income), once per instrument and month.

**Cost allocation.** A rule moves the expenses booked to a service cost centre in a month to the cost centres it serves, account by account, by weights (percentage, floor area, head count, machine hours); the split adds up to the paisa. Each rule runs once per month.

## 15.2 Project systems (done)

Migration 0066 adds `projects`, `project_wbs`, `project_milestones`, `project_cost_links`, `project_resources` and `project_time` (schema `projects.ts`). Code: `backend/src/modules/projects/` (`project-rules.ts` is pure and unit tested). Permissions: `projects.plan.manage`, `projects.time.book`, `projects.time.approve`, `projects.reports.read`. Pages: `/projects`, `/projects/:id`, `/projects/resources`.

**Projects and WBS.** A project has a customer, contract value, the service item milestone invoices are raised on, dates and a status (planning, active, closed). The work breakdown structure is a tree of elements numbered 1, 1.2, 1.2.3 (a child's code must extend its parent's), each with planned dates and a budget by cost type (material, labour, subcontract, other). Budgets, actual and committed cost roll up to the parents; an element whose actual plus committed cost is above budget is flagged.

**Costs.** Purchase orders, purchase invoices, stock issues and journals are charged to a WBS element with a cost type (a document can be charged once). Actual cost: the taxable value of purchase invoices, the value of stock issued, and the expense lines of journals. Committed cost: purchase orders less what has been invoiced against them. Labour: approved hours times the person's cost rate on the project. Drafts and cancelled documents are not counted.

**Time and resources.** People are planned on a project (and WBS element) for a date range with hours a week and a cost rate. The resource load shows each person's planned hours per week across all open projects against 48 hours a week (Factories Act), with overloaded weeks in red. Hours are booked per person, WBS element and day (at most 24 a day) and approved or rejected.

**Billing.** Milestones are shares of the contract value (together at most 100 %), with a due date. An achieved milestone is billed as a draft sales invoice on the project's billing item, which then follows the normal invoice flow. The project shows what was billed, revenue earned (contract value × earned value / budget), unbilled revenue and the margin on billing.

**Earned value.** For each leaf element: planned value (budget × share of the planned period elapsed), earned value (budget × progress entered), actual cost; for the project: CPI, SPI, cost and schedule variance, estimate at completion (budget / CPI), estimate to complete and variance at completion.

## 15.3 PLM-lite and document management (done)

Migration 0067 adds `plm_item_states`, `plm_eboms`, `cad_files`, `cad_file_versions`, and the document store tables `dms_folders`, `dms_files`, `dms_versions`, `dms_signatures` (schema `plm.ts`; "dms" in these table names means document management, not the distributor module of Phase 10). Code: `backend/src/modules/plm/` (`plm-rules.ts` is pure and unit tested; `signing.ts` holds the signing providers). API: `/plm/*` and `/doc-store/*`. Permissions: `plm.items.manage`, `plm.cad.manage`, `plm.data.read`, `docs.files.manage`, `docs.files.read`, `docs.files.sign`. Pages: `/plm`, `/plm/eboms/:id`, `/plm/cad`, `/plm/cad/:id`, `/documents`.

**Lifecycle.** Items put on a lifecycle move concept → design → prototype → released → phase-out → obsolete (with steps back for a redesign). Release is signed electronically and needs a released engineering BOM (for made items: finished, semi-finished, or with a BOM) and a released CAD file or drawing; an item cannot be made obsolete while open purchase or sales order lines use it. Phased-out items cannot be put on a purchase order, obsolete items on purchase orders, sales orders or sales invoices (checked when the document is submitted). Items never put on a lifecycle are treated as released.

**Engineering BOM.** The design parts list per item and revision (find number, part, quantity, reference designators). A draft is released under signature, superseding the previous revision; released revisions are frozen. A released revision drafts a manufacturing BOM (operations are added in manufacturing). The comparison with the approved manufacturing BOM lists design parts missing in manufacturing, quantity differences, and manufacturing-only parts such as packaging.

**CAD vault.** Each file has a number, title, item, metadata, the files it uses, a revision and a status. A new file starts checked out to its creator; only the person holding the check-out can check in a version (up to 100 MB, hashed with SHA-256). Metadata is read from DXF headers (AutoCAD version, units) and STEP headers (description, author, schema); SolidWorks and DWG files are stored with the metadata entered. A file is released under signature once checked in, and only after the files it uses are released. Checking out a released file starts the next revision (A → B … Z → AA). Where-used shows the files that use it.

**Document store.** Folders with a retention period; files with a document number, tags and versions (up to 25 MB). The text of the current version is kept for full-text search (Postgres text search with ranking and highlighted snippets) for text files and PDFs (text read from Tj / TJ operators, compressed streams inflated); scanned images and photos are not searchable without an OCR service. A file under legal hold cannot be disposed of; after its retention date it can be disposed of, which deletes the stored content and keeps the record and hashes.

**Digital signatures.** The signer confirms with their password (an electronic signature in the Part 11 chain), then the signing provider signs the SHA-256 of the version. The sandbox provider signs with an HMAC key derived per workspace, and verification recomputes it and compares the stored file hash. Aadhaar eSign (through an ESP) and DSC tokens are behind the same interface but need credentials and certificates that are not available here, so they report that they are not set up. Only the current version can be signed.

## 15.4 White-label and partners (done)

Migration 0068 adds `tenant_branding` (per workspace), `partners`, `partner_members`, `partner_commissions` (platform-wide, no tenant_id) and `tenants.partner_id`. `rls.sql` enables row-level security on the partner tables, revokes all direct access from the app role, and adds security-definer functions: `partner_of_current_user`, `partner_clients`, `partner_commissions_mine`, `partner_can_provision`, `tenant_set_partner`, `tenant_partner`, `admin_partners`, `admin_save_partner`, `admin_add_partner_member`, `admin_accrue_commissions`, `admin_pay_commissions` (the admin ones assert the platform-admin flag) and `branding_by_domain`. `provisionTenant` can now create a workspace for an existing user and a partner. Code: `backend/src/modules/whitelabel/` (`branding-rules.ts` is pure and unit tested). Permission: `settings.branding.manage`. Pages: `/settings/branding`, `/partner`, `/admin/partners`.

**Branding.** Product name, logo (PNG, JPEG or SVG data URL up to 200 KB; SVGs with scripts, event handlers or external links are refused), primary colour (white text on it must reach WCAG AA 4.5:1), support email. The app shell shows the brand's name, logo and colour; notification emails carry the product name and colour.

**Sender identities and domain.** An email sender name and address: the address is used only once its domain is proven by a DNS TXT record (`_nextgen-verify.<domain>` = `nextgen-verify=<token>`); until then mail goes from our address with the sender name. The TRAI DLT SMS header (6 letters) and principal entity id are kept for the SMS provider. A custom web address (not one of our domains, used by one workspace only) is proven the same way plus a CNAME; changing it needs a new proof. The login page of a verified domain can look up its brand without a session (`GET /public/branding?host=`).

**Partners.** Platform administrators keep partners (code, kind reseller / implementer / both, commission %, certified, active or suspended), add members by the email of an existing user, accrue commissions and record payouts. A partner member sees, in the partner portal, the partner's client workspaces (plan, subscription, users — not their data) and commissions, and creates client workspaces that they own and the partner manages. A workspace owner can also name a partner by its code, or remove it. Commission is the partner's rate on the taxable value of each paid subscription invoice of a client workspace, accrued once per invoice; payouts mark the accrued commissions paid with the transfer reference.
